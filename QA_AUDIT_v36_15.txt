CONFIRMED RECORDING ISSUES AND FIXES
1. Voice playback failure notice was a large bottom overlay, covering Progress cards and nearby controls.
   - Moved notices to a compact top position below the app header.
   - Bounded toast height and enabled internal overflow on narrow screens.
   - Shortened the unsupported-device-speech message with clear next steps.
2. Live Teacher was labelled READY before any live session existed.
   - Initial state is now IDLE / VOICE NOT CONNECTED.
   - Start Live Teacher changes to CONNECTING, then LISTENING/SPEAKING only after session events.
   - Stopping/cleanup returns to NOT CONNECTED, not READY.
3. Home ring displayed 100% under “Your Progress” while its actual formula was daily XP / daily XP goal.
   - Renamed it “Daily Goal” and clarified that it measures XP earned today, not CEFR level or overall proficiency.
